Standard Mathematical Amortization Formula

$$\text{EMI} = P \times \frac{r \times (1 + r)^n}{(1 + r)^n - 1}$$ where P is the principal borrowed, r is the monthly interest rate ($\frac{\text{Annual Rate}}{12 \times 100}$), and n is the loan tenure in months. When the interest rate is 0%, the equation simplifies to $\text{EMI} = \frac{P}{n}$.
