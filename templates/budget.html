The 50/30/20 Rule Benchmark

A proven educational baseline for personal finance:

  • 50% Needs: Housing, Food/Groceries, Bills, Healthcare (Target: ~{{ (monthly_income * 0.5)|inr }}).
  • 30% Wants: Shopping, Entertainment, Dining out, Subscriptions (Target: ~{{ (monthly_income * 0.3)|inr }}).
  • 20% Savings: Emergency reserves, investments, debt prepayment (Target: ~{{ (monthly_income * 0.2)|inr }}).

*Note: Financial rules are educational heuristics and may vary according to personal circumstances.

Zero-Based Budgeting Technique

In Zero-Based Budgeting, every rupee is allocated a job before the month starts:

  • Income − (Expenses + Planned Savings) = ₹0.
  • Surplus cash is never left unassigned, which prevents accidental impulse spending.
  • Regularly review budget allocations at month-end to adapt to variable income.
